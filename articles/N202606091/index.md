# Success for MG ZS EV in '#WhatTheBeep' rear seatbelt reminder evaluation

**09.06.2026**

![Banner](banner.png)

The [MG ZS EV](../../whatthebeep/2026-06-09-ZSEV-Executive50kWh/index.html) has today earned a rare **<span style="color: green;">PASS</span>** result in TYC's [#WhatTheBeep seatbelt reminder evaluation](../../whatthebeep/index.html).

The page administrator recently had the opportunity to test the [ZS EV](../../whatthebeep/2026-06-09-ZSEV-Executive50kWh/index.html)'s rear seatbelt reminder in person, and is pleased to report the system meets the advanced evaluation criteria of [#WhatTheBeep](../../whatthebeep/index.html).

As evident in the testing video below, the [ZS EV](../../whatthebeep/2026-06-09-ZSEV-Executive50kWh/index.html)'s rear seatbelt reminder includes occupant detection, and starts its visual and acoustic signals when an occupant has not buckled up. It does not issue any signal when a seat is unoccupied.
