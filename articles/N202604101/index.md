# Two Indian EVs in #WhatTheBeep seatbelt reminder evaluation

**10.04.2026**

![Banner](banner.png)

The #WhatTheBeep rear seatbelt reminder (SBR) evaluations for two models [have been published today](../../whatthebeep/index.html): the [Tata Punch.ev](../../whatthebeep/2026-04-10-PUNCHEV-Smart30kWh/index.html) and [Mahindra XUV 3XO EV](../../whatthebeep/2026-04-10-XUV3XOEV-AX539kWh/index.html).

Recently facelifted, the affordable electric [Tata Punch.ev](../../whatthebeep/2026-04-10-PUNCHEV-Smart30kWh/index.html) receives a <span style="color: red;">**FAIL**</span> result: its rear seats have no occupant detection. Even for a system without occupant detection, the Punch.ev presents a unique problem: while most seatbelt reminders on the market without occupant detection tend to wait for a belt to first change status (thereby failing to notify many unbelted occupants), the Punch.ev's rudimentary seatbelt reminder instead issues a second-level audio and visual signal **when any rear seatbelt is not fastened, regardless of occupancy**. These "ghost" alerts can annoy the driver to the extent of encouraging tampering with the system or perpetually keeping the rear seatbelts fastened.
