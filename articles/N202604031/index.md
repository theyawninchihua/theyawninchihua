**03.04.2026**

![Banner](banner.png)

The #WhatTheBeep rear seatbelt reminder (SBR) evaluations of four models [have been published today](../../whatthebeep/): the Mahindra XUV 7XO, new Renault Duster, and the facelifted Hyundai Verna and Exter.

Only the XUV 7XO receives a <span style="color: green;">**PASS**</span> result: it has occupant detection (Mahindra *PODS*) in all rear seats, and issues an audio and visual signal when any rear seatbelt is not fastened on an occupied seat.

The Duster, Verna and Exter receive <span style="color: red;">**FAIL**</span> results: their rudimentary rear seatbelt reminders do not have occupant detection, and only issue an audio signal when a rear seatbelt is fastened and then unfastened.

Detailed results are on the SBR evaluations page: [click here](../../whatthebeep/).
