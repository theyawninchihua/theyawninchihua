# Three more Indian cars criticised for weak rear seatbelt reminders in latest #WhatTheBeep evaluations

**18.09.2026**

![Banner](banner.png)

Today, The Yawning Chihuahua is releasing [#WhatTheBeep evaluation results](../../whatthebeep) for the rear seatbelt reminders of three Indian cars: the [Kia CARENS CLAVIS EV](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-CARENSCLAVISEV-HTX742kWh/index.html), [Hyundai AURA](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-AURA-PRIMESD12CNG/index.html), and [Toyota INNOVA HYCROSS](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-INNOVAHYCROSS-ZX20Hybrid/index.html).

## Kia Carens Clavis EV's rear seat conveniences overshadowed by a suboptimal seatbelt reminder

Launched last year, the rear seats of the [Kia CARENS CLAVIS EV](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-CARENSCLAVISEV-HTX742kWh/index.html) pamper its rear passengers with sun blinds, a tray table, and an air purifier. However, little thought has been given to its seatbelt reminder, which only chimes once a rear seatbelt is taken off while driving, the bare minimum regulations require.

With no occupant detection, the [CARENS CLAVIS EV](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-CARENSCLAVISEV-HTX742kWh/index.html) fails to audibly alert unbelted rear occupants unless they've unfastened a fastened belt. It fails two of the four [#WhatTheBeep testcases](../../whatthebeep/protocol.html), and is awarded an overall <span style="color: red;">**FAIL**</span>.

## Toyota Innova Hycross' incredibly persistent chimes don't start when needed most

During testing of the [Toyota INNOVA HYCROSS](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-INNOVAHYCROSS-ZX20Hybrid/index.html), it was revealed that the audible signal of its rear seatbelt reminder uses a more advanced kind of second-level signal, split into an intermediate warning followed by a very persistent final warning.

It is a disappointment, however, that this advanced warning does not activate when needed most: to remind rear occupants who simply haven't buckled up. Since the [INNOVA HYCROSS](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-INNOVAHYCROSS-ZX20Hybrid/index.html) cuts out occupant detection, its rear seatbelt reminder is forced to ding only when a belt changes status to unfastened, if it has to avoid false alerts. This behaviour earns it a <span style="color: red;">**FAIL**</span> in #WhatTheBeep.

## Hyundai's sedans continue string of disappointing #WhatTheBeep results

The [Hyundai AURA](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-AURA-PRIMESD12CNG/index.html) is a popular taxi. Tested in its [Prime SD guise launched last December](https://www.autocarindia.com/car-news/hyundai-india-officially-enters-fleet-segment-with-prime-hb-prime-sd-438705), the affordable sedan also receives <span style="color: red;">**FAIL**</span>, with no occupant detection and a chime only on change of belt status.

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/1EEzhE-ZYEA?si=C22r4_gBOGKoZ_dT" title="Embedded video" allowfullscreen></iframe>

The Aura's larger stablemate, the [Hyundai VERNA](../../whatthebeep/2026-09-18-VERNA-allvariants/index.html), was originally awarded <span style="color: red;">**FAIL**</span> in a #WhatTheBeep evaluation based on desktop research [earlier this year](../N202604031/index.html), which has now been audited via in-person testing. The Yawning Chihuahua is pleased to report that all testcase outcomes agree with the original result.
