Toyota's popular Hyryder SUV awarded FAIL in the simple What The Beep? rear seatbelt reminder test

20.08.2026



Make a sound whenever an rear occupant is not wearing their seatbelt, but do not for an unoccupied seat. That's ALL it takes to pass the What The Beep? seatbelt reminder evaluation.

However, like many others, the rear seatbelt reminder of the Toyota URBAN CRUISER HYRYDER manages to screw up even that, and has today been awarded FAIL in a What The Beep? in-person evaluation.

Toyota URBAN CRUISER HYRYDER evaluation explained

During testing, it was revealed that the rear seatbelt reminder of the Toyota URBAN CRUISER HYRYDER starts its secondary audible signal only when a rear seatbelt changes status from fastened to unfastened during a drive, regardless of seat occupancy.

This means the URBAN CRUISER HYRYDER fails to audibly alert rear occupants who have been unbelted from the start (likely the majority of unbelted occupants). It also falsely alerts if a belt is taken off on an empty seat (for instance, if an outboard occupant accidentally attempts to use the centre buckle).

The URBAN CRUISER HYRYDER hence fails two testcases of the What The Beep? evaluation, earning an overall FAIL.



Toyota URBAN CRUISER HYRYDER

Testcase Description Audible warning Verdict
occupant does not fasten seatbelt NO NOT OK
