### Excerpt

    From the Bayon's owner's manual:

    Rear passenger's seat belt warning

    For rear seat
    - As a reminder to the rear passenger, the rear passenger's seat belt warning lights will illuminate for about 6 seconds each time you turn the ignition switch is in the ON position regardless of belt fastening.
    - If the seat belt is not fastened when the ignition switch is in the ON position, the seat belt warning light will illuminate for about 70 seconds.
    - After wearing the seat belt, if the rear passenger unfastens it when driving under 20 km/h (12 mph), the corresponding warning light will continue to illuminate for about 70 seconds.
    - After wearing the seat belt, if the rear passenger unfastens it when driving over 20 km/h (12 mph), the seat belt warning chime will sound for about 35 seconds and the corresponding warning light will blink.
    - If the rear door is opened or closed under 10 km/h (6 mph), the warning light and warning sound do not work even if driving over 20 km/h (12 mph).
