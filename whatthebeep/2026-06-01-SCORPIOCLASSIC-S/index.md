### Excerpt

    From the SCORPIO CLASSIC (August 2025-onwards) owners' manual:

    "6.12 Passenger Occupant Detection System (PODS) & Seat Belt Reminder Indication

    PODS detects the presence of occupant in the front passenger and on 2nd row seating locations.
    Graphic
    If occupant is present on front passenger and front facing rear passenger seats and not wearing the seat belt, Seat belt reminder warning symbol lamp will glow on the vehicle instrument cluster panel and will be continuous until the seat belt is buckled.
    It should be noted that the PODS require a minimum weight hence children may not be detected by PODS. Along with this warning symbol, a chime will be ON for 30 seconds for front seat belt and front facing rear passenger seat belt reminder.
    Do keep in mind that after market seat covers also may deteriorate the occupant detection sensor performance, don’t use the non recommended/non authorized seat covers."
